# Independently specified business rules

A: Return recorded absolute schedule times; exclude past departure timestamps and cancelled services. B: Report only listed amenities. C: Enumerate directed forward stop pairs, including intermediate boarding/alighting. Enumerate up to two transfers by default, no repeated stations or trip IDs. Require departure >= previous arrival + 10 minutes, inclusive. Cancellation removes every segment of that trip. Apply delay to all stop times. These are small exhaustive fixtures, not a scalable network routing implementation.

D: A tariff must be effective inclusively on query date. Fixed, explicit stop-pair, stage-rate and distance-rate modes are independent. Apply trip override before surcharge, then only the requested eligible concession. Quantize Decimal with ROUND_HALF_UP to two places. Missing/expired prices stay null and cannot win cheapest selection. Concession fractions are synthetic policies, never official eligibility guidance.

E: Seats require an explicit snapshot. Snapshot age over 15 minutes is stale. Capacity never implies availability. F: Cancellation and delay are separate facts. G: An absent platform remains unknown. H: Missing service, unknown GPS and stale GPS have explicit warnings; no live positions are fabricated. I: Calendars apply to the first scheduled stop's operating date. Date additions/removals override weekday lists; overnight and delayed stops retain that service day. J: Ambiguous selections return all recorded candidates and a warning, rather than an arbitrary choice.

The gold suite uses ten parameter sets per case pattern. Individual scenarios store their controlling rule. Tests include Hypothesis checks for transfer ordering, directed stops, positive chronology, cancellation exclusion, nonnegative/expired fares, unknown cheapest prices, capacity independence, past departures, calendar precedence, provenance and freshness. No claim of human manual review is made. The oracle and authored expectations are separate code paths; production functions are never imported.
