# Phase 5B completion report

Date: 2026-11-08. **PHASE 5B COMPLETE** for the documented single-server, locally operated workflow. No Phase 5C or voice development was started.

## Working implementation

Operations Control Center is available at `/admin/operations` and the passenger digital board at `/board`. They read the same database-derived board: station/operator/service-date selection; arrival/departure, delayed/cancelled, platform, disruption and history views; actual counts; search, category/time-window filtering, sorting and pagination. Scheduled, estimated and actual timestamps, last update, provenance, freshness and platform kind remain separate. Past scheduled times do not imply departure or arrival.

The dispatcher engine validates boarding/ready/arrival/departure/transit/completion, delay/revised arrival or departure, platform assignment/reassignment, cancellation/suspension, reinstatement request/approval, disruption and announcement review/publication, and supervised corrections. Lifecycle, per-stop phase and delay are orthogonal. Commands require local authentication, operator/station scope, source, reason, occurred/expiry times, current version and idempotency key. Concurrent commands have one winner; exact retries replay their original event.

Existing Trip, TripStopTime, DelayEvent, PlatformAssignment, Disruption, Alert and Audit models are reused. Current projections and the append-only event audit commit transactionally. Operational audit update/delete through the ORM is blocked. Corrections append history and supersede observation projections. Standard master schemas do not expose dispatcher projection JSON/version for direct editing.

Platform intervals are checked for overlap, including other operators' confirmed assignments. Planned platform, dispatcher-confirmed assignment and explicitly observed boarding platform are distinct. Physical bay occupancy stays UNKNOWN without an actual occupancy observation. A generic assignment is never promoted to actual boarding automatically.

Reinstatement and publication of disruptions/announcements require a different station supervisor/operator administrator/system administrator. Reinstatement checks current timetable/platforms. Disruption types cover road closure, diversion, station closure, stop suspension, festival crowding, weather suspension, service interruption and other operator input. The UI provides affected service/route/trip selection, station references and start/end fields. Unpublished requests are not standard passenger information. Source validity is rechecked before publication. Announcement templates are local English JSON; a changed preview cannot be silently published.

Same-origin local WebSocket invalidations use a monotonic audit revision and one-second database checks, with no broker. Clients ignore duplicate/older revisions, detect disconnect/missing heartbeat, reconnect with backoff, refetch authoritative state and retain 15-second polling/manual refresh. Private pending payloads and actor details are not exposed over the public stream.

Existing passenger enquiries and the Phase 3 planner consume the same projections. Cancellations/suspensions and actual departures are excluded from available journeys; revised verified stop times change journey/transfer calculations. Delay NLP answers include the revised departure and confirmed platform. Actual arrival/departure still requires a recorded observation. Expired estimates are not revived when a new actual event is recorded. No GPS position, weather, traffic or automatic prediction was fabricated.

## Known limits and deployment inputs

- No authoritative operational feed or APSRTC dataset was supplied. Both main-app acceptance services and platforms are explicitly DEMO. Stored demonstration station is Vijayawada; physical PNBS identity, actual platform numbering and official operations are not certified. Staff must publish real local reference/source data before operational use.
- This is a single-server local system, not a multi-site broker/replay cluster. WebSocket revision checks invalidate all station boards on operational changes and refetch authoritative REST data; scale under large simultaneous client counts is not certified. The launcher binds loopback; LAN exposure/TLS/firewall policy is separate explicit deployment configuration.
- Board queries cap candidate services at 5,000; REST results paginate. Platform lists cap at 500 bays/200 visible assignments per bay; operational quality examines up to 2,000 observation rows. UI reference lists show up to 100 rows; broader scopes/large histories use existing paginated APIs/explicit local IDs. Console history shows the latest 100 scoped events; REST history is paginated.
- ETA is manual, with no invented probability/confidence score. A verified local dispatcher observation is distinct from official-source provenance. GPS and physical platform occupancy remain UNKNOWN when absent. No automatic platform release or geofencing is inferred from the clock.
- Stop dispatch currently addresses a station visit through the service's active dated variant; repeated visits to the same physical station require separate operational service/variant modelling. Arbitrary historical event rewriting/replay is not offered. Supervisor correction targets the latest trip event at the station and preserves earlier history.
- One dated Trip per service/date is the existing schema contract. Multiple festival departures need separately numbered services/calendars. Date selection is origin service date, not a merged rolling station-day board across all previous service dates.
- Operations events are append-only at the application/ORM layer, not a cryptographic tamper-evident store against a database administrator. Emergency database recovery is a deployment responsibility.
- Disruptions report verified local affected scopes; they do not automatically fabricate a new diverted timetable. Publish the corresponding route/calendar changes through Phase 5A when needed. Booking/payment/refund/voice, predictive ETA, external tracking and Phase 5C were not added.
- The statistical intent candidate remains unapproved. The tested production flow uses the existing deterministic English fallback with the operational phrase correction. No multilingual/voice readiness is implied.

No substantial implemented workflow is represented by a mockup or empty handler. Setup/limits and the original requirement mapping are in PHASE_5B_SETUP.md and PHASE_5B_AUDIT.md.
